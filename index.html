Read to Me

Paste markdown. Listen to it.

Your text shows up as a clean page and gets read aloud by a natural voice that runs on this computer. The part being read is highlighted, and you can click any paragraph to jump there.

About the voice

It runs on your computer

The voice is Kokoro, an open source speech model. It runs inside this page, so there's no account, no API, and no cost, and your text never leaves this computer. The only thing downloaded is the model itself.

The first load is the slow one

The first time, the page downloads the model from Hugging Face: about 325 MB on computers that can use the graphics chip, or about 92 MB otherwise. Your browser keeps it, so later visits start in seconds.

Where it works

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari, and every browser on iPhone and iPad, have trouble running this model.

Saving as MP3

Save as MP3 reads the whole document with the voice and speed you have selected and saves it to your downloads folder. You can keep listening while it works.

One track per section saves a zip of MP3s, one per heading, tagged as an album. Unzip it and drag the folder into the Music app, and each section becomes a track you can skip between. One file with chapters saves a single MP3 with chapter markers, which some podcast and audiobook players show and many others ignore.

Picking a voice

Heart and Bella are the highest rated voices. The male voices are rated lower. Changing the voice restarts the current sentence in the new voice.
